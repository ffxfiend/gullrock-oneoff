How They Came Together

Balasar Norixius was summoned to the capital by his father, and no son of the Norixius Clan travels without an escort. In a frontier trading post he hired Feng, a towering young half-orc who asked for little pay and seemed to know every trail by smell. A day later they were joined by a charming merchant's widow seeking safe passage north, who introduced herself as Lady Celise Aubren.

Three days out, the group found Althaea Starflower arguing with a pair of woodcutters over a felled tree. Feng stepped in before it turned ugly, and the elf, who was also bound for the capital with business of her own, decided the company was worth keeping. She has spent most of the trip since debating Balasar about whether nobility is a natural law.

They are still a few days from the capital when they come upon a weary fisherman resting by the road.

Mara's player decides when, or if, the others learn her real name.

Why They'd Take the Job

  • Balasar: A noble's duty is to protect the common folk, and a heroic deed would look very good in a letter home.
  • Feng: A ruined fishing ground is a wound to the natural world, and legends are made by answering calls like this.
  • Mara: There's a reward, and a village elder is exactly the kind of person worth getting close to.
  • Althaea: Something unnatural is poisoning the sea, and she's curious what it is.

How They Came Together

It started with a card game at a roadside inn. A cheerful halfling tea merchant named Pip Merriweather was winning far too often, and a pair of angry teamsters were about to do something about it. Kildrak, who had just shared a prayer with "Pip" over supper (she claimed to be a devoted follower of the Silver Flame), loudly vouched for her honesty. Aramil, reading by the fire, set down his book and asked each side to explain their position in full, which confused everyone long enough for Nulara to step between them and calmly suggest the teamsters go to bed. They did.

Over breakfast the four discovered they were all headed to the capital: Kildrak to report to his order's high temple, Aramil to visit its great library, Nulara to settle affairs left over from her army days, and Pip to wherever the purses were fattest. Safety in numbers seemed wise.

They are a few days into the journey together when they come upon a weary fisherman resting by the road.

Vani's player decides when the others learn her real name.

Why They'd Take the Job

  • Kildrak: Helping folk in need is the Silver Flame's work, and a good deed done well is noticed by those higher up.
  • Vani: There's a reward, and a desperate village is an easy place to find a friendly game.
  • Aramil: A mysterious slime ruining the fishing grounds? He has never read about anything like it.
  • Nulara: People are suffering and asking for help. She won't walk past that.

Pick the Exotic if...

  • You want unusual races and a bit of edge.
  • You like characters with tempers and grudges.
  • You want more magic and more healing.

Pick the Classic if...

  • You want the iconic fantasy party.
  • You're newer to D&D and want clear roles.
  • You want a rogue for stealth and traps.
